Winter
market.

Saturday, December 13, on the town square. From nine in the morning until the light goes.

Forty makers

Bread, wool, cider, clay, and one very good cheese van.

Free entry

Dogs welcome, on leads. Kids run free.

Held outdoors

Dress for the weather. Mulled things available.

Bring a bag

Stall map at the gate. Card and cash both fine.

Rain or shine
